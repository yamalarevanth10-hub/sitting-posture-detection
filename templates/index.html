Methodological & Mathematical Formulation

  • Shoulder Tilt: $\theta_s = |\arctan2(\Delta y, \Delta x)| \times \frac{180}{\pi}$ relative to horizontal plane.
  • Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR): $EAR = \frac{||p_2 - p_6|| + ||p_3 - p_5||}{2 \times ||p_1 - p_4||}$ via 6 facial landmarks per eye.
  • Slouch Index: $R_{slouch} = \frac{\Delta y(\text{Head}_{\text{mid}}, \text{Shoulder}_{\text{mid}})}{\text{Distance}(\text{Left Shoulder}, \text{Right Shoulder})}$ normalized for depth invariance.
